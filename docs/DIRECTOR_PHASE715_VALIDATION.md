## Interpretation

The 115 Director phases are concrete TypeScript filmmaking operations rather than roadmap-only contracts. The isolated acceptance gate proves deterministic source behavior and coverage. It does **not** yet prove final rendering quality, native application behavior, GPU performance, or Square Media-level artistic output. Those claims require restored dependencies, a complete build, native tests, visual review and at least one rendered golden short film.
